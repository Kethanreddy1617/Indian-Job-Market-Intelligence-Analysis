# Indian Job Market Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing 97K+ job postings from the Indian job market.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Power BI
- Tableau
- Excel

## Key Analysis
- Top job titles
- Top hiring companies
- Major job locations
- Salary analysis
- Experience requirements
- Company ratings

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze Indian job market trends and provide insights into hiring demand, popular job roles, locations, salaries, and experience requirements.

## Dashboard Features
- Total job postings
- Total hiring companies
- Average salary
- Average company rating
- Top 10 job titles
- Top 10 hiring companies
- Top 10 locations
- Jobs by experience
- Interactive filters
